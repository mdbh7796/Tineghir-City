Food & Drink

Tagine, Medfouna, oasis dates, mint tea in Tinghir — try Chez Mohamed, La Kasbah, and the patisseries on Avenue Mohammed V. Bottled water is easiest.

Money & Costs

Dirhams only — ATMs on Avenue Mohammed V. Souk prices negotiable.

Language

Tamazight first; French widely understood. Try azul and tanemmirt.

Etiquette

Dress modestly, ask before photos, accept the tea.

Quick Answers

When is the best time to visit?

March–May and September–November: warm days, cool nights, full river.

How many days do I need?

One day for gorge + palm grove. Three days add Todra Valley villages and palmeraie walks.

Is it safe for solo travelers?

Calm and welcoming. Normal precautions: water, sun, insurance.

Do I need a guide?

Not for town or gorge walks. Worth it for climbing routes and village visits with a local guide.
