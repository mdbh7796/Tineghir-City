Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Contact: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}

No analytics, no ads, no account. This app collects nothing about you. There is no tracking SDK, no advertising identifier, and no server receiving your behavior. The consent prompt stores only your choice (`granted` / `denied`) on your own device.

Location stays on-device. Sort-by-distance and “locate me” read GPS only to center the map and order nearby places. Coordinates never leave your phone and are never uploaded, shared, or sold.

Maps. Online map tiles come from OpenStreetMap servers (standard HTTP logs on their side apply while the online layer loads). The bundled offline map works without any network. See tile attribution on the map itself.

Storage. Saved places, packing list, theme, and consent choice live in on-device local storage. Clear site/app data to erase them.

Children. The guide contains no user-generated content, chat, or purchases and is suitable for general audiences.
