Flights & Transport

Buses, grand taxis, and petit taxis — every way in and around Tinghir.

Arriving in Tinghir

Tinghir has no airport of its own — arrive via the nearest airports (Ouarzazate OZZ, about 3 hours; Errachidia ERH, about 2 hours), then continue by bus or grand taxi into town. Everything below starts once you are in Tinghir.

By bus

🚌

CTM

Comfortable intercity coaches from the town station. Book a day ahead in spring and autumn.
