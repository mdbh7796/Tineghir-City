Flights & Transport

Fly into the south, ride the buses, and haggle a grand taxi — every way in and around Tineghir.

By air

✈️

Ouarzazate (OZZ)

The film city airport, about 3 hours by road through the Dades Valley — the scenic arrival.

✈️

Errachidia (ERH)

The closer airport, about 2 hours east — fewer flights, shorter transfer.

Comfortable intercity coaches from the town station. Book a day ahead in spring and autumn.
