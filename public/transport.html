Flights & Transport

Fly into the south, ride the buses, and haggle a grand taxi — every way in and around Tineghir.

By air

✈️

Ouarzazate (OZZ)

The film city airport, about 3 hours by road through the Dades Valley — the scenic arrival.

Google Flights Skyscanner
✈️

Errachidia (ERH)

The closer airport, about 2 hours east — fewer flights, shorter transfer.

Google Flights Skyscanner

By bus

🚌

CTM

Comfortable intercity coaches from the town station. Book a day ahead in spring and autumn.

Book on CTM
🚌

Supratours

ONCF's coach network covering the south. Tickets at the station or ONCF counters.

By taxi & car

🚕

Petit taxi

Fixed short routes around town for a few dirhams (~5 MAD base). Agree longer fares first.

🚙

Grand taxi day hire

For the gorge, Dades Valley, and villages. Agree the day rate before departure.

📞

Call transport

Numbers and advice in one place, available offline.
