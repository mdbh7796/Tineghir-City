Transport

Petit taxis

Fixed short routes around town for a few dirhams (~5 MAD base). Agree the fare before longer rides.

Grand taxi day hire

For Todra Gorge and palmeraie villages inside Tinghir. Agree the full-day rate before departure; splitting with other travelers is normal.

CTM / Supratours station

Intercity buses leave from the town station. Book a day ahead in spring and autumn.

Health

Pharmacies

Day pharmacies open roughly 09–21h on Avenue Mohammed V; nights are on call — ask your hotel or any pharmacy door sign.
