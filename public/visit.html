Plan Your Trip

Visit Tineghir

The best time to visit is during spring (March-May) or autumn (September-November) when temperatures are pleasant for exploring.

✈️

Getting There

Fly to Ouarzazate or Errachidia airports, then drive through scenic routes to Tineghir.

🏨

Where to Stay

Choose from traditional riads, kasbah hotels, or modern accommodations with stunning views.

🗣️

Local Tips

Learn a few Berber phrases, dress modestly, and always negotiate prices in the souks!

Quick Facts

Region: Draa-Tafilalet

Language: Berber, Arabic, French

Currency: Moroccan Dirham (MAD)

Climate: Semi-arid, hot summers

Best Season: Spring & Autumn
