## Full description

Discover Tineghir, the gateway to Morocco's majestic Todra Gorge.

- Offline-ready maps with GPS locate-me, plus online OpenStreetMap tiles
- Must-see attractions: gorge, palm groves, kasbahs, souks, valleys
- One-day and three-day itineraries, practical prices, emergency numbers
- Where to stay: book, call, or WhatsApp riads and kasbah-hotels
- Trip tools: packing checklist and saved-places planner, all on-device
- Dark mode, English interface, no account, no tracking

Location permission is optional and used only on your device to centre the map
